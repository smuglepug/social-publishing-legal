Privacy Policy

Social Publishing Automation · Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains how the Social Publishing Automation tool (“the App”) handles information. The App is a private, self-hosted publishing tool operated by a single individual to publish their own video content to their own social media accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public and has no other users.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by an individual creator for personal use. Contact: smuglepug@gmail.com.

2. What information the App accesses

When you connect a social media account to the App, it accesses only:

  • Account authorisation tokens issued by the platform you connect (for example TikTok), which allow the App to publish content on your behalf.
  • Basic account identifiers returned by the platform, such as an account ID or display name, used to confirm the App is posting to the correct account.
  • Content you publish through the App, meaning the video files and accompanying titles, descriptions and tags that you choose to upload.

The App does not collect analytics, does not access your followers, messages, contacts, or viewing history, and does not obtain your platform password.

3. How information is used

  • Authorisation tokens are used solely to upload and publish your content to your own connected accounts.
  • Account identifiers are used solely to verify the destination account before publishing.
  • Content is transmitted only to the platform you direct it to.

Information is never used for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond publishing your own content to your own accounts.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.
  • We do not use your information to train machine learning models.
  • We do not transfer your information to advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not post content that you did not explicitly direct the App to post.

5. Where information is stored

Authorisation tokens and content files are stored locally on the operator’s own computer, on the operator’s own storage. They are not uploaded to any third-party server, cloud service, or database operated by us. Transmission to a platform occurs directly between the operator’s computer and that platform’s official API over an encrypted connection.

6. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are retained only while the connected account remains linked to the App. You may revoke access at any time from your account settings on the relevant platform, or by deleting the stored credential file on the operator’s computer. Revoking access immediately ends the App’s ability to publish. To request deletion of any stored credential, contact the address in section 1.

7. Third-party platforms

Content published through the App becomes subject to the terms and privacy policies of the destination platform (for example TikTok, Meta, or YouTube). This policy does not govern those platforms’ own handling of your information.

8. Children

The App is not intended for, and is not made available to, anyone under the age of 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the App. The “Last updated” date above will be revised accordingly.
